One word for my Wi-Fi: **Ghosting**. The signal is there mid-sentence, mid-download, and then it is simply gone — no warning, no goodbye. It borrows the language of bad dating and applies it to a router: a network that ghosts you is funny at once, true to how home Wi-Fi actually behaves (it vanishes at the worst moment), and fresh where the field will flood with "slow", "buffering" and "dead". It is also a network name people would smile at every time they connect — which is the point, since the winning word becomes the sponsor's network name.

